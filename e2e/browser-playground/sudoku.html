A few useful controls

  1. Click a white square and type a number from 1 to 9. Shaded clues stay fixed.
  2. Use arrow keys to move between squares. Backspace or Delete clears your entry.
  3. The number pad also fills the last square you selected.
  4. Check progress highlights incorrect entries without filling in the answers.
