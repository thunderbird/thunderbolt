Pick any square. Your first reveal is always safe.

How to play

  1. Click a square to reveal it. A number tells you how many mines touch that square.
  2. Right-click to place a flag, or turn on Flag mode and use a regular click.
  3. Reveal all 71 safe squares to win.

Keyboard friendly
Tab to a square; Enter or Space reveals it. Press F on a focused square to toggle its flag.

“Play Minesweeper. Start at row 1, column 1 and explain your next move.”
